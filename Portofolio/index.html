Hi! This is a few of my work over the year, i have many more project aside from these, but i deem these three to be the most important ones

Web-Based Examination for the Visually Impaired

This is my Final Thesis project, it is a Web-Based Examination Application that utilize Text-to-Speech and Speech-to-Text technology into it. All the navigation (aside from the login) is done via Speech-to-Text

E-HRMS

E-HRMS is a Web-Based database management application that is meant to be used by HR to make it easier to oversee and manage which employee is in need of training

Maling no Swiping

Maling no Swiping is a mobile side-scrolling endless runner game made with Unity. You follow the story of a Final Year ITB student that got his laptop stolen which have all his final project files in it to chase down the thief who stole it
